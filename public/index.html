Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

Is posting a requirement free?

Yes. Buyers can post requirements and view quotes for free.

Which cities does Civix cover?

Currently Indore, Bhopal, Jabalpur and Gwalior.

How does a vendor get verified?

Our admin reviews each vendor before approval, and verified vendors get a blue tick.

How is payment handled?

There is no online payment on the platform yet. Payment and delivery are agreed directly with the vendor after you accept a quote.
